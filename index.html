The Story Behind The Cake Cottage

Rasmita has always loved trying new things in the kitchen — from Mughlai to Thai, Punjabi to Korean, she taught herself one dish at a time. Her food won over competitions, family and friends long before it became a business.

At just 18, armed with a small auto oven and homemade ingredients, she started baking for fun. That hobby slowly grew into a passion, and today, The Cake Cottage is where her love for baking finally has a home of its own.
